Terms and conditions

Last updated: 30 September 2026
EmberWatch is a demonstration prototype. It is not an emergency dispatch system, a certified fire detection service, or a substitute for local verification.

Service description

EmberWatch presents satellite thermal observations with industrial asset context, screening scores, and downloadable evidence briefs. Features, data coverage, and availability may change without notice.

Demo and sample data

Some records are curated sample observations created for judging and evaluation. Labels such as suspected industrial fire are screening outputs, not confirmed incidents. Users must verify events with appropriate local, scientific, or operational sources before taking action.

Acceptable use

You may use the console for lawful evaluation, research, education, and internal demonstrations. Do not use it to make an immediate safety-critical decision without independent verification.

Prohibited use

Do not attempt to disrupt the service, bypass access controls, abuse third-party data services, present sample data as official intelligence, or use the prototype to target people or organisations unlawfully.

Limitations and availability
