Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026
This page is a prototype disclosure for the EmberWatch demonstration. Replace the contact placeholders before production use.

What this service collects

EmberWatch is a browser-based monitoring prototype. The public console does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, passwords, payment information, or precise device location. If an operator contacts the project team, the information included in that message is received for support and evaluation.

Operational data

The service processes NASA FIRMS observations, industrial asset context, and derived risk scores. In the hosted demonstration, these records are used to render the console and may be held temporarily by the hosting provider. Demo observations are sample scenario data and must not be treated as an operational incident record.

Local storage and cookies

The console uses browser local storage only to remember the selected colour theme. It does not set advertising cookies. Hosting, security, or map providers may process technical request data under their own policies.

Third-party services

The prototype may request data from NASA FIRMS, OpenStreetMap tile services, Esri satellite tiles, Google Fonts, and the Render hosting platform. Those providers may receive an IP address and standard request metadata when your browser contacts them directly.

Use, retention, and security

Information is used to operate, troubleshoot, evaluate, and improve the prototype. Retention depends on the hosting configuration and source provider. No retention period is promised for this prototype. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security or availability.

Your choices

You can clear the console’s local storage through your browser settings. For questions about information held by the project, contact [project contact email]. Requests involving third-party source data may need to be directed to that provider.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the prototype changes. The date above identifies the current version.
